Our mission

Our firefighting mission is to become a fully autonomous partner to fire and rescue services: spotting fires before they get out of control, and finding people who are lost or hurt on the mountain.

Two problems, one drone

What we're here to solve.

01

Fires, caught before they spread.

Our drones patrol high-risk slopes through the fire season and pick out the first signs of smoke and heat, even at night. Fire crews get the exact location and a live feed of how the fire is moving, so they can reach it while it's still small.

02

People, found faster.

When a hiker goes missing or someone is hurt on the mountain, the drone searches from above, day or night. Thermal vision picks out a person where the eye can't, and rescue teams are guided straight to them.

The drone

Built to get there first.

Every Tambora drone is designed and built by us in South Africa, and flown by TamboraAI, the brain that lets it fly itself and understand what's happening on the ground. It doesn't wait for someone to see the smoke. It's already in the air.

Always on

Patrols the fire season

Flies high-risk areas day and night when the danger is highest. When its battery runs low, it lands on a dock that swaps the battery automatically and sends it straight back up.

Sees

Spots smoke early

Picks out the first signs of smoke and heat, even at night or through haze. It knows what a normal slope looks like, so a new fire stands out straight away.

Searches

Finds people from above

Sweeps the mountain in a pattern, using thermal vision to find a lost or injured person where a search party on foot would take hours.

Reports

Live to the crews

Sends fire and rescue teams the exact location, a live feed and how the fire is moving, so they know where to go before they set off.

Supervised

Safe by design

Flies itself, overseen by licensed remote pilots who can step in at any moment, and stays clear of firefighting aircraft. Every flight follows South African aviation rules.

Learns

Smarter every season

What one drone learns today, every drone knows tomorrow. Each season teaches TamboraAI more about how fires start and spread here.

Where we start

Eyes on the mountain.

We start by making sure no fire goes unseen for long and no one lost on the mountain waits alone. Our first firefighting drones deploy in 2028, patrolling high-risk slopes through the fire season and searching the moment someone is reported missing.

Every flight teaches TamboraAI more about our mountains: how fires start, how they move in the wind, and where people tend to get lost.

Where we're going

Fighting fire after dark.

Today, when the sun goes down, firefighting helicopters are grounded, and a fire on a mountain can grow through the night until they can fly again.

We're building towards drones that keep going after dark: carrying water to a fire through the night, when helicopters can't, and holding it back until the crews and aircraft return at first light.

Every drone we fly today brings that future closer. Our goal is simple: no fire should get the chance to spread, and no one should be left alone on the mountain.
