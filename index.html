Every fire deservesa head start.

Autonomous drones that spot fires early and help bring people home safely.

A fire that's spotted in its first minutes can be stopped. One that's found an hour later can take a mountainside, and the homes below it. Our firefighting mission is to become a fully autonomous partner to fire and rescue services: spotting fires before they spread, and helping find people when they're lost or in danger.

It starts on the slopes and in the veld. Our drones patrol high-risk areas through the fire season, spot smoke early and alert fire crews with the exact location and a live feed. When someone goes missing on a mountain, the same drones search from above, by day and by night.

First deployments · 2028

The drone

Built to get there first.

Every Tambora drone is designed and built by us in South Africa, and flown by TamboraAI, the brain that lets it fly itself and understand what's happening on the ground. It doesn't wait for someone to see the smoke. It's already in the air.

Always on

Patrols the fire season

Flies high-risk areas day and night when the danger is highest. When its battery runs low, it lands on a dock that swaps the battery automatically and sends it straight back up.

Sees

Spots smoke early

Picks out the first signs of smoke and heat, even at night or through haze, with thermal vision. It knows what a normal slope looks like, so a new fire stands out straight away.

Searches

Finds people from above

When a hiker goes missing or someone is trapped, it searches the mountain from the air, using thermal vision to pick out a person where the eye can't.

Reports

Live to the people who need it

Sends fire and rescue crews the exact location, a live feed and how the fire is moving, so they know where to go and what they're facing before they arrive.

Supervised

Safe by design

Flies itself, overseen by licensed remote pilots who can step in at any moment, and stays clear of firefighting aircraft. Every flight follows South African aviation rules.

Learns

Smarter every flight

What one drone learns today, every drone knows tomorrow. Each season teaches TamboraAI more about how fires start and spread in South Africa.

Where we start

Seeing the smoke first.

We start by making sure no fire goes unnoticed for long. Our first firefighting drones deploy in 2028, patrolling high-risk slopes and veld through the fire season and alerting crews the moment smoke appears.

Fire crews see the fire live before they set off, so they know its size, which way it's moving and how to reach it. When someone is lost or hurt on a mountain, rescue teams get eyes in the sky straight away. Every flight also teaches TamboraAI more about our landscapes.

Where we're going

A future where fires never get the chance.

Today, a fire on a mountain can burn for an hour before anyone sees it, and a search for a missing hiker can take a whole day. We're building towards a South Africa where both are measured in minutes.

First, drones that do more than watch: dropping water or fire retardant on a new fire before it can grow, and carrying supplies to people stranded on a mountain until rescuers reach them.

Then, fleets that work alongside fire services through every season, mapping the fire line in real time, guiding crews safely, and keeping watch over the places people live, hike and farm.

Every drone we fly today brings that future closer. Our goal is simple: no fire in South Africa should get the chance to spread.
